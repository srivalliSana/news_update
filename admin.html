Post to publish

In the watched channel, just post a message — the first line is the headline, the rest is the body, and any attached photo is pulled in automatically:

TCS Campus Drive Results #Placements #breaking
30 students were selected today with a ₹3.6 LPA package.
Congratulations to all the students! 🎉

Edit the Slack message → the article updates. Delete it → the article disappears. Section hashtags: #Campus #Academics #Events #Sports #Placements #Research #Achievements #Alumni. #breaking marks a notice; #featured pins it to the hero.

⚡ Slash Commands to Register

Register these in Slack App → Slash Commands. Replace the base URL with your public server URL (e.g. from ngrok).

CommandRequest URLDescription
/publish—Post a new article
/list-news—List recent articles with IDs
/archive-news—Archive an article by ID
/delete-news—Permanently delete an article

Slash command format

/publish Campus | Library Wing Opens | The new 3-floor library was inaugurated today. | Editorial Desk
/publish Placements | TCS Drive | 30 students selected with ₹3.6 LPA package. | Placement Cell | https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1521737604893-d14cc237f11d?w=800
